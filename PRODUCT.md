# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Men of all ages and boys (children's cuts) in and around Skien. Many come in from Torggata in the town centre; they either call ahead or walk in. They want a good cut, a tidy beard or a classic shave at a fair price, and need the phone number, address, prices and hours fast, usually on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Sadoun Barbershop AS is a barbershop at Torggata 15, 3717 Skien (org.nr. 833 475 142, established March 2024, small team). The website exists to make people in Skien call or drop in: it shows the services and prices, opening hours, address and the phone number 46 15 91 57. Success is a call or a visit, not time spent on the page.

## Positioning

Confirmed by the owner side: precise fades and cuts, beard work and classic wet shaves, and good prices for the quality. Not claimed: awards, years of experience, named barbers, or anything not confirmed.

## Operating Context

Customers book by phone, and walk-ins are welcome. There is currently no online booking. Visitors are local and mostly on mobile; Norwegian first, with an English toggle.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site (plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step) in this repo.
- Language: Norwegian by default, English via toggle.
- Primary action: call 46 15 91 57 (`tel:+4746159157`). Secondary: see prices, open the address in maps.
- Open product decisions: real prices (current ones are examples), real opening hours (current ones are examples), online booking link (none yet), social profiles (none supplied).

## Brand Commitments

The name is Sadoun Barbershop. The user asked for a classic retro feel. No logo or other brand assets have been supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

Public registry facts only: address, phone, organisation number, founding date and manager name (Brønnøysundregistrene, 1881.no). Absent and not to be fabricated: photos of the shop or work, a logo, reviews or testimonials, confirmed prices, confirmed opening hours.

## Product Principles

1. Calling or walking in is the goal: phone number, address and hours are never more than one scroll or tap away.
2. Say only what is confirmed. Placeholders stay clearly marked until the owner supplies real prices, hours and photos.
3. Craft and fair price are the claim: precise cuts and beards at good value, not luxury or hype.
4. Built for a phone in someone's hand in the town centre, then for desktop.
